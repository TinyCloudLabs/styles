Sharing a space with a link

A share link carries a scoped, expiring delegation. The recipient opens it, the viewer verifies the proof, and only then is any content rendered. See the verification notes for the full flow, or call shares.create() from a script. Press Ctrl + C to copy the link.

How a link is resolved

Resolution happens in three steps. Each step can fail on its own, and the failure state names the step that rejected the link.

  1. Parse the fragment and decode the delegation.
  2. Verify the chain of proofs:
    • issuer matches the space owner;
    • expiry is in the future and nbf has passed;
    • the requested resource is covered by the scope.
  3. Fetch the content and render it in a sandboxed frame.

What the recipient sees

Identity, scope, and expiry stay visible above the content, so the decision to trust the page can be made where it is needed.

  • Sender and space, shown as short identifiers.
  • The allowed action, such as tc:kv/get.
  • Time remaining before the link stops working.

Before sending a link, check that:

  • The scope covers only the files you mean to share.
  • The expiry is as short as the recipient allows.
  • The recipient has confirmed the channel it arrives on.

Links are bearer credentials. Anyone who holds the full URL can open the content until it expires, so share them like passwords.

Example request

const link = await tc.shares.create({
  space: "did:key:z6MkhaX4F9",
  path: "reports/q3.md",
  actions: ["tc/kv/get"],
  expiresIn: "7d", // a link that never expires is rarely what you want
});

console.log(link.url);

Why can a link fail to open?

The proof chain is invalid, the link has expired, or the scope does not cover the requested path.

Questions about a specific link? Open it, then choose Inspect proof to see every delegation in the chain.
